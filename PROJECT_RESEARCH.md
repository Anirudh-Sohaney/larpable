## Types

Every record receives top-level `type` derived from its source opportunity posts: `nonprofit`, `project`, or `company` (company/startup). This preserves original author classification rather than inferring legal status from public research. For grouped posts, types must agree or migration fails before mutation. PULSE Karachi remains separate from pharmaceutical PULSE.
