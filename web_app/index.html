FAQ

Common questions

Is Larpable free?

Yes. Making a profile, browsing, applying and posting your own projects are all free for students.

Who is Larpable for?

Students who want to share what they're working on, find opportunities, or find teammates for their own projects.

What kinds of opportunities are there?

Student projects looking for teammates, plus internships, research roles and projects posted by professors and companies. Each listing says what the project is, who posted it, and the role and skills they're looking for.

Can I look around before signing up?

Yes. Browse opportunities as a guest and read the details. You'll need an account to apply or post.

Can I post my own project?

Yes. Share what you're building, explain where you need help, and students who are interested can apply to join.

What shows up on my profile?

The projects and updates you post, the work you've shared, and your LarpScore, which grows when other students engage with your work.
